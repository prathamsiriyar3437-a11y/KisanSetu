Value-Chain Bottleneck

When the Crop is Ready, Logistics are Not.

Post-harvest loss is not merely a transport availability failure—it is a coordination, timing, and capacity visibility gap.

⚖️

Fragmented Micro-Loads

Smallholders producing 200–500 kg cannot economically charter an entire 3-ton truck, forcing reliance on middleman distress pricing.

⏱️

Perishability Clock

High-perishable horticulture suffers quality and weight decay every hour spent stranded in unconditioned yard conditions.

📵

Connectivity & Access Gap

Smartphone-only solutions alienate marginal farmers. Systems must operate seamlessly via SMS, USSD, and assisted FPO counters.
